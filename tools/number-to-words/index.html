Writing numbers on cheques

On a cheque, write the whole amount in words followed by the cents as a fraction over 100 — for example One thousand two hundred fifty dollars and 75/100. Draw a line through any unused space to prevent alterations.

How to use it

  1. Type the number.
  2. Choose words or cheque format, currency and case.
  3. Press Convert and copy.

FAQ

How do I write 55388 in words?

Fifty-five thousand three hundred eighty-eight.

Does it support decimals?

Yes — as 'point' digits in word mode or as a /100 fraction in cheque mode.
