The 9-year cycle

Numerology moves in nine-year cycles. Year 1 plants seeds, year 5 brings change, year 8 harvests results and year 9 completes the cycle. Your Personal Year = your birth month + birth day + the current year, reduced to one digit.

How to use it

  1. Enter your birth date.
  2. See your personal year, month and day.
  3. Plan big moves in years 1, 5 and 8.

FAQ

When does a personal year start?

Most numerologists use January 1; some use your birthday. Our calculator uses the calendar year.

What is a personal day?

Personal year + current month + current day, reduced — a quick daily theme.
